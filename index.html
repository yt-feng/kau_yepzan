FAQ

Answer the common questions up front.

Wix pages often bury practical details. A quick FAQ keeps this version clear and conversion-friendly.

How do I edit this website?

Change the text directly in index.html, update colors in styles.css, and adjust the small interactions in main.js.

Can I add images or a gallery?

Yes. Add your image files to the repository and swap the placeholder content in the highlight blocks for <img> elements.

Where can I host this cheaply?

GitHub Pages is free for public repositories, and Cloudflare Pages or Netlify are also good low-cost options.
