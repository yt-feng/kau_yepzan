Method

What makes the teaching different

1. Start from how Chinese was built

Instead of giving learners disconnected phrases, the method starts from where characters come from, how sounds are organized, and how words are formed. This makes later reading and memorization much more logical.

2. Build a plan around the learner's real goal

Some students need university support, some need conversational Chinese, and some need business outcomes. The practice course is structured around the destination rather than forcing everyone through the same path.

3. Keep support available between formal sessions

The WhatsApp-based assistance model lowers learner friction. Students do not need to wait for the next class to get unstuck, which helps keep momentum high.
