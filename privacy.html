Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Information we receive

The site may receive information that you choose to send by email, such as your name, email address, contact details, learning goal, project details, or timeline. The short-video practice interface stores learning progress locally in your browser so the feed can remember your progress.

Payments

When you enroll in a course, Paddle processes payment, billing, tax, and transaction information under its own privacy terms. tiktalk academy does not ask for or store full card details on this static site.

How information is used

Information is used to answer inquiries, prepare course access, provide requested support, maintain the learning experience, and address payment or refund questions. We do not sell inquiry details.

Third-party content

Short-video lessons may load media from their published source hosts. Those hosts may process technical information under their own policies.

Contact and choices

For privacy questions or a request about an inquiry, email info@tiktalk.ac. You can clear local learning progress through your browser’s site-data controls.
